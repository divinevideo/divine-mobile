# Divine 1.0.28

More ways to play with a six-second video, easier sound browsing, and fewer
interruptions from likes and reposts.

## Make something people can join in

- Add polls, quizzes and emoji sliders that viewers can vote on.
- Invite video responses with Add yours and replies stickers. Question stickers
  let viewers answer privately when your account can receive direct messages.
- Draw with neon, calligraphy and rainbow brushes.
- Try new color looks, spin blur, swirl, ripple and fluted-glass effects, plus
  8-bit, 3D glasses and riso-print looks.
- Speed up or slow down an added sound over time.

## Find your sound

- Browse sounds with cover images and a music/sound-effects filter.
- Preview saved sounds right from My Sounds in the Library.

## Notifications that give you room

- Likes and reposts can arrive together in one summary instead of a burst of separate alerts.
- Divine can use app visits to reset when likes and reposts can notify you again,
  and avoid interrupting you while the app is open.
- Opening the app does not mark your notification history read.
- Direct messages do not use up the limit for likes and reposts.
- Tap a summary to catch up in Notifications.

Visit-based timing is not available with every sign-in method. Some external
signers cannot report app visits and keep a rolling notification limit instead.
If you use Amber, you may need to reconnect it and approve the requested
permission before visit-based timing works.

## Keep going without the hiccups

- Fixed an Android startup hang after tapping a notification.
- Direct messages are less likely to go missing, and retracted messages no
  longer stay visible when their deletion arrives before the message.
- Switching accounts keeps the refreshed sign-in session.
- Edits to untitled videos can now publish, and a rejected edit shows an error
  instead of reporting success.

## Downloads and rollout

The direct Android downloads reuse the tested 1.0.28 beta files without rebuilding.
Google Play: not publicly released yet; build 847 is in internal testing.
App Store and macOS availability for 1.0.28 is not confirmed here.
Store-installed apps may show an update prompt before their store has this version.
