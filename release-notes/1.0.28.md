# Divine 1.0.28

Fewer interruptions, with likes and reposts grouped into notification summaries.
Tap a summary to catch up in Notifications.

## Notifications that give you room

- Likes and reposts can arrive together in one summary instead of a burst of separate alerts.
- Divine can use app visits to refresh your notification allowance and avoid interrupting you while the app is open.
- Opening the app does not mark your notification history read.
- Direct messages do not consume the nonurgent notification allowance.

## Downloads and rollout

The Android downloads reuse the original signed 1.0.28 production-backend beta artifacts.
Google Play build 847 is available in internal testing; production promotion is being prepared.
iOS and macOS availability is not confirmed by these Android downloads.
Store-installed apps may show an update prompt before their store has this version.
