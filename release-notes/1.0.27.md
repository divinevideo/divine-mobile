# Divine 1.0.27

More ways to make a six-second video your own: new effects, moving masks,
voice treatments and stickers people can tap. Finding your way around the
editor is easier, too.

- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.openvine.app):

- Direct Android downloads:

## More room to play

- **Find your look faster.** Effects and filters are grouped into categories.
- **Let it rain.** Add rain, leaves, balloons and more particle effects,
  alongside light leaks, tilt-shift, zoom blur and comic looks.
- **Move the picture.** Pan, zoom and turn a clip over time with keyframes.
  Animate a shape mask to reveal a face, wipe across a clip or move a spotlight,
  and choose the color behind a masked clip.
- **Make an entrance.** New transitions include light leak, clock wipe,
  diagonal wipe, flip and cube. Text and stickers can spin and flip in and out.
- **Shape the sound.** Add reverb, phone, megaphone, underwater or slowed +
  reverb treatments to a sound track. Auto captions now include voice-over
  takes and audio extracted from clips, as well as the clips' own speech.
- **Fine-tune the image.** Adjust highlights, shadows, vibrance and sharpening.

## Stickers with something to say

- Add mentions, hashtags, links and sound credits that viewers can tap on a
  published video. External links show their destination before opening.
- Add time, date, weather, place and other info stickers, including a camcorder
  recording timer. Choose a place by name for place-based stickers.

## Find, share and keep going

- Profile videos use a three-column grid that keeps each video's proportions.
- Native video and profile sharing can use short links. If shortening is
  unavailable, sharing keeps the full link.
- Profile pictures and banners stay visible after app startup.
- Direct-message retries avoid repeatedly sending the same message or reaction.
  Sharing stays responsive while delivery continues, and unreadable older
  messages show an explanation instead of an empty bubble.
- Aligned loops keep a fixed view, and restored clips no longer start another
  repair every time the library opens.
