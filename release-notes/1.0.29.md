# Divine 1.0.29

More ways to make a six-second video your own, better control over comments,
and fewer bumps between an idea and a finished loop.

## Make something a little weirder

- Let a drawing draw itself, stroke by stroke, with the new Draw on animation.
- Pick a color straight from your video for drawings, text and other editor tools.
- Try smoother slow motion with the optional frame-blending setting.
- Give captions an outline, a glow or a highlight that follows each word.
- Set stop motion to take a frame every few seconds while you build the scene.

## Your comments, your call

- Hide a comment on your own video without blocking its author. You can show it again later.
- Comments from people you block stay out of your comment threads.
- Find current trending hashtags in Popular instead of an old fixed list.

## Keep the good bit going

- Camera and editing controls work better with large system text on small phones.
- Improved sign-in recovery after an interrupted account switch.
- Fixed interrupted video-cache saves and errors when closing the editor.
- Improved reporting from direct messages.

## Downloads and rollout

These notes accompany the direct Android downloads. Google Play, App Store and
macOS availability for 1.0.29 is not confirmed here. Direct downloads and store
rollouts happen separately; store-installed apps may show an update prompt
before their store has this version.
