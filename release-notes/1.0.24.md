## More ways to make a scene.

We've been busy in the editor and around it since 1.0.23. Here's what's new to try.

## In the editor

- **Make a title move.** Titles can now type themselves out, wiggle, bounce, or loop. Pick the motion and let it go.
- **Blur or pixelate the bit you want hidden.** Draw over anything in the frame and soften it, or block it out.
- **Land effects on the beat.** Time your visual effects to the music so the moment hits where you wanted it to.
- **Draw at the size you meant.** Set the brush size before you start drawing.
- **Keep flashing gentle.** Flashing effects hold to three flashes a second, even across the loop point, and still carry the flashing-lights warning when you post.

## Make a scene together

- **Hand off your raw clips.** Share a camera-verified clip so a friend can cut it into their video, and keep camera-verified takes in your clip library.

## Watching and messaging

- **Details under the name.** Turn on video loops or the publish date in Settings, and they now show under the creator's name while you watch.
- **Captions with a lighter touch.** Captions blur the video behind them, and a tap, double-tap or hold on a caption now reaches the video underneath.
- **Room to breathe.** The Home feed picker uses the full width of its row.
- **Avatars look right.** A picture with see-through parts no longer shows what's behind it, and a profile picture no longer flashes a placeholder as it opens.

## Fixes you'll feel

- **Stay signed in** when the login server has a rough moment.
- **Reactions and messages behave.** Unsent reactions and removals retry while the app stays open, a failed removal shows your reaction again, and a message reads as delivered only once it reaches an inbox the recipient actually reads.
- **Playback is steadier.** Pausing near the end sticks, and a late preroll no longer pauses the video on its own.
- **Lists hold together.** People lists load and save again, blocked or muted accounts stay out of a list's members, and removing someone sticks.
- **Crossposting controls work.** Disconnect and Reconnect are real buttons, and we sign in to the crossposter the right way.
- **Content settings follow the creator.** Age gates and adult-content choices are respected on uploads.

Thanks for testing along with us. Spot something? Send it from Settings → Support Center.
